GlucoPal: Non-Invasive Glucometer

Finalist, BRAINSTORM 2025
  • A portable glucose monitor that uses near-infrared (NIR) sensing and photoplethysmography (PPG) instead of finger-prick tests.
  • Captured raw PPG signals and built the processing pipeline (filtering, normalisation, feature extraction) on a Raspberry Pi.
  • Designed and trained a ResNet34-based model in PyTorch to predict blood glucose from processed PPG waveforms.
