Research

Research experience

My research covers human–computer interaction, medical imaging and 3D perception. The common thread is machine learning applied to signals from real people and real environments.

  1. Dec 2025 – Jun 2026

    Visiting Research Student

    AID Lab, School of Computer Science, The University of Sydney · Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Anusha Withana

    Research area: Human–Computer Interaction.

    Australian Sign Language (Auslan) dataset & recognition

    • Led the end-to-end development of an Auslan dataset to help hearing parents learn to communicate with their deaf children. This covered data collection, coordination with external parties and organising the collection process.
    • Developed and compared deep learning architectures for sign language recognition on the collected data.
    • Co-authored a conference paper, currently under peer review.

    MotionMAE: human motion prediction

    • Studied and evaluated human motion prediction models using IMU sensors placed at different body locations.
    • Ran sensitivity and noise analyses on how sensor placement and measurement noise affect prediction performance, plus ablation studies on the framework’s components.
  2. Jul – Nov 2025

    Research Intern

    Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) · Supervisor: Prof. Na Zhao

    Topic: 3D Domain Generalisation for Autonomous Driving.

    • Researched domain generalisation for 3D object detection in autonomous driving, with a focus on cross-domain robustness.
    • Worked with the nuScenes and Argoverse 2 datasets using the CMT and FSDv2 codebases.
  3. Jul – Nov 2025

    Research Contributor, LiverUSRecon

    University of Moratuwa · Supervisor: Prof. Ranga Rodrigo

    Project: Automatic 3D reconstruction and volumetry of the liver from a few partial ultrasound scans.

    • Contributed to medical image analysis research on 3D organ reconstruction and volumetry.
    • Tested multiple liver ultrasound datasets and ran experiments extending the approach to the kidney.
    • Started with TransUNet and moved to LeViT-UNet for better performance.
